# Portable review and detector requirements

This is a sanitized consolidation of the campaign-operations and records-analysis skills. It is a requirement set, not an implementation or current legal opinion. The retired archive is excluded. Later owner decisions override obsolete skill scheduling and broad low-value closure text.

## Identity, coverage and stage truth

Preserve exact original bytes by SHA-256; retain every delivery occurrence and its context. Hash identity is not authenticity. Locators include page/region, sheet/cell/row or MIME part, parser/version and original identity. Count originals, occurrences, extracted units, unique events and reviewed pages separately.

A digest states its denominator, actual read scope, observations with locators, assertions, contradictions, cross-record relationships and gaps. Parsing/OCR and visual rendering do not prove original-level review. Keep source-first independent challenge separate from authoring and legal/privacy roles.

## Seven detectors

| Detector | Required evidence and calculation | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| search-before-training | Stable user join; timezone-aware searches versus training completion and operative requirement | Missing roster production is not proof of untrained access |
| purpose-quality | Qualified purpose categories and denominator; per-user and overall; explicit empty/whitespace/redacted/generic/case-only distinctions | Produced blank is not automatically a native blank; nonblank does not prove authorization |
| external-sharing | Recipient identity/jurisdiction and distinct permission/query/result/disclosure events | Enabled access is not completed disclosure |
| retention-over-policy | Same data class, units, effective interval, settings, policy/contract and exceptions | Detection stores, logs, evidence, backups and exports differ |
| audit-gap | Required frequency versus produced completed audits in a defined production period | Missing production is a documentation gap, not established nonperformance |
| cpra-deadline | Verified receipt/determination/extension events, governing rule revision and sourced holiday calculation | Determination deadline is not a blanket production deadline |
| volume-anomaly | Deduplicated events by user/day/local time against a declared baseline | Triage only; never a standalone violation claim |

Historic severity thresholds (including purpose percentages and recipient flags) are prioritization proposals, not law. Apply only to qualified observations and resolve conflicts with redaction/unknown handling before implementation. Each detector must be pure, deterministic, versioned and independently testable without network/model calls.

For each input and each detector retain evaluated/skipped/blocked outcome and why; zero hits with unknown coverage is not an all-clear. Persist exact locators and private observed values; public summaries omit raw identifiers. Re-evaluate on relevant source, extraction, join, rule or detector changes.

## Event-date law and local policy

Use official primary authority, jurisdiction, regulated role, mandatory/advisory status, precise clause, effective interval, exceptions and amendment history. Record retrieval date separately. Operational conduct, withholding decisions and retention events may each select a different date/rule.

Potential research families include California ALPR and public-records statutes, applicable local policies/codes/resolutions, contracts and incorporated terms, retention schedules and specifically triggered federal/state rules. These are research leads. Do not assume every rule family applies or that a draft law package is independently reviewed.

Distinguish agreement templates, signature-like marks, footer dates, actual execution and operative adoption. Distinguish policy promises from completed audits. Preserve contrary evidence, no-conflict results and unresolved versions. No technical validator adjudicates a legal violation.

Canonical classifications:
NOT_ASSESSED; NOT_APPLICABLE; VERSION_OR_APPLICABILITY_UNRESOLVED; NO_CONFLICT_OBSERVED; APPARENT_RULE_CONFLICT; CONFIRMED_RULE_CONFLICT; CONFIRMED_DOCUMENT_CONTRADICTION; DOCUMENTATION_GAP; PRODUCTION_SCOPE_GAP; REDACTION_OR_EXPORT_LIMIT; AGENCY_ASSERTION_UNCORROBORATED; LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED.

Compatibility aliases must not upgrade meaning. Confidence labels are separately recorded; they are not counsel signoff.

## Review tiers and receipts

Tier A is a narrow documentary update: independently reproduce its facts and review exact public content for privacy. Do not smuggle a rule-conflict allegation into tier A.

Tier B needs factual, legal and privacy challenge under the full records contract, with at least two distinct reviewers beyond the author covering those roles. Stable identity must be bound to actual authorized reviewers, not arbitrary self-asserted IDs. One qualified reviewer may cover two roles; the author cannot independently approve their own claim.

Receipts bind source/claim/artifact identities, actual reviewed locators and coverage, role, verdict, rationale and an explicit-timezone timestamp. Give the independent reviewer neutral sources before the desired conclusion where possible. Preserve disagreement and superseding corrections. Changed bytes or material source/rule context invalidates affected approval.

Structural gate success is not substantive factual, legal or privacy clearance. A diagnostic gate without physical file checks cannot establish publication readiness.

## Privacy and owner readiness

Store public content separately from private manifests. Inspect full public text, attachments, metadata, links and map data, including indirect identification from one row or combined facts. No raw plates, private names, home/contact details, case-linked personal data, badge IDs, signatures or sensitive precision in public outputs without lawful reviewed treatment.

Review and approval consume the same immutable public snapshot. Undeclared attachments are not covered. A byte change reopens the affected gates. owner_ready is not approved; approved is not deployed. Public source URL may be null until a reviewed public copy exists.

## Gaps and follow-up drafts

Draft targeted requests identifying the existing request, missing category, likely custodian/system, period, usefulness, prior disposition and least-burdensome alternative. Consider segregable metadata, existing native exports/data dictionaries and rolling production without inventing a right to a new substantive analysis.

Reconcile fresh correspondence before sending. Document opposing legal arguments; do not assume every exemption fails or invent appeal rights, deadlines or sanctions. Escalation and all external sending remain per-item owner decisions.

## Rendering and OCR

Use local approved parsers/renderers. Record every page, method/version, derivative identity and unsupported/error outcome. Confidence exists only if actually supplied by the method. Full-page image recovery is not OCR; OCR is not visual review. Do not improvise unreviewed decoders or execute embedded content. Preserve originals and failed attempts.
