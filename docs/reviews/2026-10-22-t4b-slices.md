  invariants, `blocking` rules, apply gate (`resolution_policy_mismatch`, `resolution_policy_changed`, `resolution_plan_blocked`

- Tests: `test_mail_adoption_resolution.py` (23), `test_mail_adoption_resolution_acceptance.py` (1), all pass.
  Existing `test_mail_adoption*.py` (70) pass unchanged. Full suite: 3277 tests, same 35 failures + 2 errors, nothing new.
  `tools/check_public_tree.py`: findings `[]`. `scripts/scan-secrets.mjs`: PASS.
- Covered design tests: 24-31, 33-37a, 37c, 37d, 37e (partly), 40, plus the pure-plan order independence.
- Not covered (open):
  - 32 concurrent root writer.
  - 37b kill after R1 commits and before its preservation stage inside a chain (slice 2 covers the single-receipt crash).
  - 37e with a valid v1 local-capture row. Only an unreadable stand-in row (fails closed) and a pure-plan case are tested.
  - 35 builds the fixture once and reverses discovery order, not two builds with chosen file names.
  - The valid-v1 and "fixture built twice" cases need a local-capture fixture that does not exist yet.
- Open questions (defaults taken, all marked RECOMMENDED in the design):
  1. Census is a flag on `adopt-mail`, not a separate command. Left to the implementation review.
  2. Policy hash comes from the config (option 1), not an installed constant (option 2).
  3. A configured but unmatched receipt blocks instead of falling back to `original_scope_unresolved`.
  4. `after` gets the resolution keys only when a policy is loaded, so existing reports stay identical. Top-level
     keys are always present (`resolution_policy.state = none` without a policy). Design 8.3 does not say which.
  5. New safe codes not named in the design: `resolution_policy_bytes_mismatch` (policy bytes differ from the receipt),
     `original_bytes_conflict` (ledger bytes differ), `original_resolution_mail_kind_mismatch` (census, EML with a non-eml format).
  6. A dependent receipt that also holds a held original keeps its own accepted `original_scope_unresolved`.
