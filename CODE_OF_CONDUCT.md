# Community conduct

Be respectful, precise, and willing to correct mistakes. Disagreement about evidence
is welcome; harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, fabricated evidence,
impersonation and disclosure of private data are not.

Critique policies and documented conduct without targeting private individuals.
Use public issues for non-sensitive moderation concerns; ask maintainers for a private
channel for sensitive reports. Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict
participation and explain appropriate corrective action.
